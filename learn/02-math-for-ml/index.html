Math for Machine Learning

Linear algebra, multivariate calculus, probability distributions, information theory (entropy, KL divergence), and optimization (SGD, Adam).

Key Topics Covered

  • Vectors, matrices, matrix multiplication, rank, eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
  • Partial derivatives, jacobians, hessians, gradient vector calculation.
  • Bayes' theorem, continuous and discrete distributions, expectations and variance.
  • Loss surface topology and gradient descent optimization (SGD, Momentum, Adam).
